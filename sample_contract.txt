SERVICE AGREEMENT

Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement to any third party without the prior written consent of the other party, and any change in ownership of the Customer is treated as an assignment.

All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the addresses written at the top of this Agreement. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
